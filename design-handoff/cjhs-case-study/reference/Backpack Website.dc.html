We learn your business before we market it.

Most agencies hand you a questionnaire and a template. We spend the first stretch of every engagement doing something closer to a job shadow.

1

We get inside it.

We sit with you, your staff and your numbers. How a customer finds you, what they ask, what they pay, what you turn away. By the end we can explain your business the way you would.

2

We find the thing that makes you different.

Your customers already know why they chose you over the competition. Most businesses have never put it into words. We find it, test it against what your market actually responds to, and make it the foundation of your positioning, your creative and your offer.

3

We write the strategy for you alone.

Not a package, not a tier. The channels, the offer, the creative and the budget are set by what we learned in the first two steps, and we walk you through the reasoning before anything runs.

4

We become part of your team.

We are an extension of your in-house team, and you can always reach us. Most of the time you will not need to. We are already in your campaigns, testing, refining and finding the next gain, so every month is better than the last. Your growth is the job.

Who We Are

Backpack is a Vancouver marketing agency built by people who have spent their careers selling and building marketing for businesses of every kind. We run campaigns, content, websites and recruitment advertising for clients across Canada and the US, from restaurants and retailers to manufacturers, senior living operators and national employers, and for the past two years we have also been the production team behind other agencies, building the work they sell to their clients. That side of the business taught us to deliver on scope, on time, every time. The direct side is where we get to go deeper: learning a business from the inside, finding what makes it different, and building marketing around that instead of around a template.
